FAQ

Quick answers.

Which course is right for me?

First Gear if you've never driven, Test Ready if you've had lessons and want to get test-ready, and Back on the Road if you already have your licence. Not sure? Message me and I'll point you to the right one.

How much are lessons?

£45 for a 1-hour lesson. A 10-hour block is £430, which saves you £20, and the 20-hour intensive course is £990. Refresher and motorway lessons are £45 an hour, and Pass Plus is £350.

Do you pick up from uni or halls?

Yes. Pick-up and drop-off from home, student halls, campus or work across the areas I cover.

Do you teach automatic?

Manual only. A manual licence lets you drive both manual and automatic cars, so you get the most freedom once you pass.

How many lessons will I need?

Everyone's different. The DVSA says learners who pass take around 45 hours of lessons plus 22 hours of private practice on average. After your first lesson you'll get an honest estimate.

What do I need before my first lesson?

Just your provisional driving licence. You'll need to pass your theory test before you can book the practical test.

Can I use your car for my test?

Yes. You'll get a warm-up lesson around the test routes first, then take your test in the car you learned in.
